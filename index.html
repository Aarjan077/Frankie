02 / BEHIND THE LENS

About Frankie.

Photography has been part of my world since I was young.

MY STORY SO FAR

What started as an early passion led me to study BA (Hons) Photography at Bournemouth University. Through university and personal projects, I've developed my practice in photography and moving image, with a particular interest in cinematography and visual storytelling.

WHERE I'M HEADING / FILM & TELEVISION

My next chapter is behind the camera.

I'm looking to begin my career in a camera or lighting department across film and television. I'm keen to keep learning and developing my skills in a professional production environment.

CURRENTLY OPEN TO

Production Runner / Camera Trainee / Lighting Trainee

Available for short-notice work across the UK
ALWAYS IN PROGRESS

For me, photography isn't a finished chapter. There's always more to learn, more to see, and something meaningful still to create.

— FRANKIE / BEHIND THE FRAME
